## Our Mission

Computers For Kids, Inc. is a nonprofit organization working to bridge the digital divide by providing access to refurbished computers and technology for individuals and communities in need.

## What We Do

- **Technology Access** — Making computers more accessible to people who need them
- **Education & Opportunity** — Helping students and families access essential digital tools
- **Community Impact** — Supporting digital inclusion and stronger communities
- **Electronic Waste Reduction** — Giving technology a second life through refurbishment and reuse
